Location

Far enough out that the phone stops being interesting.

We meet in a hall on Haywood Street, twelve minutes from a trailhead. Afternoons are unscheduled on purpose — most people walk.

Venue
Kingsfield Hall, 118 Haywood Street, Asheville, North Carolina 28801. Four hundred and eighty seats, one floor, no balcony.
Doors
Friday 6 March, 6:00 p.m. through Sunday 8 March, 1:00 p.m. The room opens an hour before each session and stays open after.
Afternoons
Nothing is programmed between 1:00 and 5:00. Trail maps and a list of quiet places are in the welcome packet.
Most people drive

Travel

Getting here takes some effort. That part is deliberate.

The drive in is the first quiet hour of the weekend. Leave earlier than you need to and take the parkway for the last stretch.

By air

Asheville Regional (AVL) is twenty minutes south of the hall. Charlotte (CLT) is a two-hour drive and almost always the cheaper ticket.

By road

Two hours from Charlotte, three and a half from Atlanta, five from Nashville. Street parking on Haywood is free after six.

Where to stay

Rooms are held at three inns inside a ten-minute walk. Ask for the Come Away rate when you book.

Block releases 14 February

Closes 20 February or when the room fills

Register

Take the three days.

There is no ticket and nothing to pay. Kingsfield covers the room, the food and the coffee — we only need to know you are coming, so there is a seat and a meal waiting for you.
