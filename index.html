Line-up

The ministering line-up.

Fikayo Olu-Ayeni

Author  ·  Conference speaker  ·  Pastor, NewSong Assembly, Alexandria, VA

Fikayo Olu-Ayeni is an author, conference speaker and pastor at NewSong Assembly, in Alexandria, VA, alongside his wife, Kofo.

He is known for his simplistic, practical and relatable approach to teaching the Word of God and pastoral counseling. His life’s mission is to know God, be known by Him, be known for Him and to make Him known.

He enjoys quiet long walks (preferably scenic), travel and spending time with his wife and extroverted daughters.

Deji Olu-Ayeni

Pastor, Lighthouse Church, Ottawa

Deji Olu-Ayeni serves as pastor of Lighthouse Church in Ottawa alongside his wife, Bukky. He is called to raise a generation of priests unto God and kings who reflect God’s dominion and influence in every sphere of society for His glory.

Deji considers it a profound privilege to shepherd God’s people. He is passionate about seeing lives transformed through the power of God’s Word, teaching biblical truths with clarity, simplicity, and practical relevance while inspiring hope and purpose in people from all walks of life. He relies wholeheartedly on the Holy Spirit to bring lasting life change to everyone they have the opportunity to serve.

Beyond ministry, Deji and Bukky are devoted parents to their two children and balance the demands of family life with thriving professional careers. In their leisure time, they enjoy traveling, watching movies, and following sports, creating meaningful memories together with family and friends.

John Wallace

Church planter  ·  Teacher  ·  Spiritual father

John Wallace has given more than five decades to ministry as a church planter, preacher, teacher and spiritual father. He serves on the boards of several large international ministries, and his life’s work has been the development of people — built on personal relationship rather than platform ministry alone.

Present during the Toronto Blessing, he has spent years learning to walk in the power and presence of God through firsthand experience of healing, renewal and miraculous works.

Today his focus is spiritual fathering — raising leaders who mature in faith and then turn to mentor others — out of a settled conviction that leadership is not about influence, but inheritance.

Dammy Olu-Ayeni

Lead Steward, Prevailing & Borderless Church

Dammy Olu-Ayeni is a disciple and follower of Jesus Christ. He serves as the Lead Steward of the Prevailing & Borderless Church which, in just 4 years, has grown into an active global community of over 26,000 subscribers and followers online. He is also the Lead Steward of the Hang Out With JESUS Global Revival Movement. He is deeply passionate about soul winning and has a special evangelistic call of God upon his life.

In the corporate world, Dammy is an accomplished Wall Street investment banking executive with a proven track record of advising top Fortune 500 CEOs & Boards. Dammy is currently Managing Director at Santander Bank USA. Prior to that, he was a Managing Director at Bank of America in New York. And before that, he was Senior Vice President for Bank of America with coverage responsibility for 4 nations in the Middle East & Gulf region. He has on-the-ground banking experience in New York, Dubai, Charlotte, Johannesburg and Lagos, Nigeria.

Dammy resides in the New York tristate area. He is happily married to his wife Ope and they are blessed with two children.
